First public release of MicroDeck.

MicroDeck turns your Android phone into a magnifier for soldering and fine work. Pick the macro, main or telephoto lens, zoom, focus by hand or with autofocus, adjust exposure and white balance, turn on a grid overlay, light your work with the torch, and save snapshots to your gallery.

No accounts, no ads, no telemetry. The only network request is the update check against this repository, and you can turn it off on the About screen.

Requires Android 13 or newer. Download MicroDeck.apk below; MicroDeck.apk.sha256 lets you check the download.
